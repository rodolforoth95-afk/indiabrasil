Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for 6 months: no joining fee, no card details and no commission, as long as your registration is valid (a verifiable company and contact).

What happens after 6 months?

We tell you in advance and show you the plan and its price. If you don’t want to continue, nothing is charged and your profile can be removed. Since we never ask for a card, nothing is charged without your decision.

How many RFQs will I receive?

It depends on your product category and on demand from Brazilian buyers. We only send requests that match your products, so you spend time on real opportunities.

Does India Brazil buy my products?

No. We are a sourcing concierge. You sell directly to the Brazilian buyer and agree price, payment terms and shipping with them.

How are payments made?

Directly between you and the buyer, on the terms you agree, such as advance TT or Letter of Credit. India Brazil never collects money on your behalf.

Do I need Brazilian certifications?

Not to register. Some categories, such as pharma, food, cosmetics and medical devices, need Brazilian approvals before sale. We will tell you what applies to your products.
